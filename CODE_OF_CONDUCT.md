# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in Underpass AI
projects a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- empathy and kindness
- respect for differing opinions and experiences
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- taking responsibility and learning from mistakes

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- harassment or discrimination of any kind
- insulting or derogatory comments, trolling or deliberate disruption
- public or private abuse
- publishing private information without explicit permission
- other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Enforcement

Maintainers clarify and enforce these standards and may take any action they
deem appropriate in response to unacceptable behaviour.

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces and when an individual
represents a project in public spaces.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the repository
maintainers through GitHub. Every report is reviewed and investigated promptly
and fairly.
